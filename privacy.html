Privacy Policy

Wake Up Alex. Last updated: November 21, 2026.

Wake Up Alex is a puzzle game published by MATRONIKA Kft. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

No account, no personal profile

The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any login. Your progress, silver, streak and settings are stored only on your device.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. To confirm a purchase and to restore it on a new install we use RevenueCat, which receives a random app user ID, the product bought, the price, the store transaction ID, the country of the store and basic device information. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Gameplay analytics

To make the puzzles better we collect anonymous gameplay events: which puzzle was opened, how long it took to solve, which hints were used, and where players stop playing. These events are linked to a random installation ID, not to you. We use PostHog to store them. See the PostHog privacy policy. We do not use the data for advertising and we do not sell it.

Crash and store data

Apple and Google may share anonymous crash reports and download statistics with us according to the settings on your device.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the data described above does not identify anyone.

Keeping and deleting data

Data on your device is removed when you delete the game. Anonymous analytics are kept for at most 24 months. To ask for deletion of purchase or analytics records tied to your installation, write to us at the address below.

Your rights

If you are in the European Union or a country with similar law, you can ask us for access to, correction of or deletion of data about you, and you can complain to your data protection authority.

Changes
